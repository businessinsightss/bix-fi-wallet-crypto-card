01 / ACCESS

Know your recovery options.

Understand the steps required if you lose a device or cannot access your account. Keep recovery information private and follow the provider's documented process.

BIX.fi crypto card

Explore the payment offering.

The website describes a crypto-backed credit card using USDT or USDC collateral. It advertises Apple Pay and Google Pay support, plus virtual, physical and metal card tiers. Confirm availability for your location.

Area What to Check Why It Matters
Account eligibility Residence rules, identification and provider requirements. Available services can depend on your circumstances.
Collateral terms How assets are committed, obligations settled and funds released. These conditions affect access to allocated assets.
Usage costs Applicable card, currency, transaction and withdrawal charges. Your typical activity determines the practical cost.
Payment support Procedures for refunds, disputed transactions and declined purchases. You need a clear process when a payment needs attention.

Separate wallet access from card obligations.

When reviewing any combined product, examine the agreement for each service. Wallet access, collateral arrangements and payment disputes may involve different procedures. Ask for clarification before committing funds.
